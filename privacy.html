Privacy and data protection

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how NIMX-G AI HUB intends to handle account information, conversations, and other information associated with using the platform.

We aim to handle information responsibly, explain our practices clearly, and protect information through appropriate security measures.

Last updated: October 27, 2026

IMPORTANT: SUBSCRIPTION AND AI SERVICE WARNING

Subscription payments help maintain and operate NIMX-G AI HUB's AI services.

Operating an AI platform can involve costs for AI provider usage, computing resources, storage, security, maintenance, and other technical services. Subscription revenue helps us cover these expenses and continue improving the platform.

Your subscription is a payment for the plan and access described at the time of purchase. It is not a donation unless explicitly identified as one.

  • Each subscription plan may have daily limits, monthly allowances, image-generation limits, or other restrictions.
  • A paid subscription does not automatically provide unlimited messages, unlimited images, or unlimited access to every AI model.
  • AI providers may change their availability, pricing, technical requirements, or usage limits.
  • We may need to adjust features or usage allowances to maintain service reliability and manage operating costs. Material changes to paid-plan benefits should be communicated appropriately.
  • Before paying, carefully review the current price, duration, included features, usage limits, and applicable renewal and refund terms.
PLEASE READ BEFORE PAYING.

Do not subscribe on the assumption that every AI service will always be free for the platform to operate, that every provider will remain available indefinitely, or that a subscription guarantees unlimited usage.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on which features are implemented and used, the platform may process the following information:

  • Account information: information such as your email address, username, and account identifier.
  • Profile information: information you choose to provide, such as your display name, date of birth, or optional biography.
  • Conversations: messages and responses associated with your saved chat history.
  • Uploaded content: text, images, or files you submit when using a feature that accepts them.
  • Technical information: limited information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.
  • Subscription information: information needed to identify a plan, confirm payment status, and manage subscription access, where those features are implemented.

We should only collect information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. The information actually collected depends on the features and integrations in operation.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage user accounts.
  • Provide and maintain platform features.
  • Save and retrieve conversations when chat history is enabled.
  • Process AI requests through the relevant service providers.
  • Verify payments and manage subscription entitlements.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Protect accounts and investigate abuse or security problems.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

Information should not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or other required permission.

3. AI Providers and External Services

NIMX-G AI HUB is designed to connect to third-party AI services. When these integrations are active, information you submit may need to be transmitted to the relevant provider to generate a response or perform a requested task.

Different providers may have different privacy policies, retention practices, processing locations, and terms. Their handling of submitted information is governed by their applicable policies and agreements.

We will aim to use only the information needed to fulfil a request. Users should review the applicable provider terms and avoid submitting passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information unless a feature genuinely requires it and suitable safeguards are in place.

4. Chat History and Stored Information

Where conversation storage is enabled, chat messages may be associated with your account so that you can revisit previous conversations.

Stored conversations may remain available until they are deleted or removed under the platform's applicable retention rules. Deleting a conversation from the interface may not immediately remove copies held in backups, security logs, or third-party systems, where such copies exist and are lawfully retained.

The platform should provide clear information about available deletion controls and any applicable retention periods.

5. Account Security and Confidentiality

We aim to use appropriate safeguards to protect account information and restrict access to authorized purposes. Access to private account information should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for operation, support, security, and legal compliance.

No website, database, internet transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute confidentiality or protection against every possible security incident.

Users should protect their passwords, avoid sharing account credentials, and report suspected unauthorized access.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate a feature you request, process a payment, provide hosting or storage, maintain security, or deliver support.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to address security threats, fraud, or abuse.

We should not make private account information or conversations publicly accessible without an appropriate basis or the user's informed authorization.

7. Payments and Subscriptions

If subscriptions are introduced, payments may be processed through a third-party payment provider. The payment provider may process information necessary to complete and verify transactions.

NIMX-G AI HUB should retain only the payment-related information needed to manage subscriptions, confirm transactions, provide support, and meet legal or accounting obligations.

Payment-card details should be handled by the authorized payment provider rather than stored directly in public website files or ordinary chat records.

Subscription prices, billing periods, cancellation procedures, refund rules, and plan limits should be disclosed before the user completes a purchase.

8. Cookies and Technical Storage

The platform and its connected services may use browser storage, session information, cookies, or similar technologies to maintain authentication, remember necessary preferences, and operate selected features.

The actual technologies used depend on the website's implementation and its connected services. Any non-essential tracking technologies, if introduced, should be explained and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law and the features available, you may be able to request access to your information, correct inaccurate details, delete eligible information, or raise an objection to certain processing activities.

Some information may need to be retained for legitimate security, legal, transaction, or operational reasons. Any such retention should follow applicable requirements.

10. Children's and Young Users' Privacy

Users must satisfy the applicable age requirements for the platform and its connected AI, hosting, and payment services. Different providers may impose different minimum-age or parental-consent requirements.

Users must provide truthful information when age verification or eligibility checks are required. We should not knowingly collect information from children in ways prohibited by applicable law.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the platform introduces new features, changes service providers, or needs to reflect legal or operational changes.

Material changes should be communicated through an appropriate notice or other suitable method. The updated policy should display its revision date.

12. Contact and Support

If you have questions about this policy, your account, or the handling of your information, visit our Help & Support page.
